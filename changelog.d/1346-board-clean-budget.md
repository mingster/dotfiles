* `board-clean.py` stops at its round budget. `BOARD_CLEAN_BUDGET_S` sets a deadline at the start of `main()`. Every subprocess runs through one helper that lowers its timeout to the time left and never starts under 1s from the deadline. A call cut short by the budget raises `OutOfTime`: the card or host is left alone with no board comment, the script prints the budget line and its summary, and exits 0 (a call that ran its own full timeout is still a normal failure). Fixes `board-clean` exiting 124 every round (#1346, refs #1303).
