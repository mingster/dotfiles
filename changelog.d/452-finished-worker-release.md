- `worker-finish.sh` and `worker-pane-close.sh` treat native `worker-release --json` as the only lifecycle mutation: strict boolean `ok`, CLI exit, exact requested `dispatchId`, and `state`/`processAction` before worktree removal or clearing pane records. Retained, pending, malformed, id-mismatch, and missing-dispatch receipts fail closed; dirty, conflict-unmerged, or uninspectable worktrees are kept after release. Worktree removal requires proven `HEAD` ancestry in trusted `origin/main` or an exact merged PR head, including blocking clean worktrees whose unique commits are not yet in main; ancestry or PR queries that fail retain the worktree. Pane cleanup verifies native disappearance and never calls generic `terminal close`.
