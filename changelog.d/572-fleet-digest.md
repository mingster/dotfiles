fleet-watch no longer types anything into a terminal. Notices queue and go out as one orchestration message per terminal at most every 10 minutes; a dead lead pane, a failed dispatch, a dead waiter or a host over cap goes at once as a message, and a Verdict goes at once to the owning lead. New `script/coordinator-drain.sh` removes "Worker stopped without reporting" for leads from the coordinator's inbox check and fleet-watch reports the count per lead in its digest. fleet-watch reads host caps and load ceilings from `fleet-config.json` (default `~/.claude/state/fleet-config.json`, `fleet_config` key for tests) instead of `host_caps` in its own config. A missing or unreadable file refuses admission and sends one notice. `on-worker-done.sh` reads the same file by default. The coordinator's waiter runs `FLEET_WATCH_CONFIG=<private-json> script/coordinator-drain.sh`, with the same config fleet-watch gets; a config with no `leads` key is reported on stderr instead of dropping nothing quietly.
