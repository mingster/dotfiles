`worker-done-relay.sh sweep`, run once per fleet-watch pass, relays each unread `worker_done` to its owning lead (read with `check --peek`, once per dispatch) and releases the seat only on proof: a builder's branch on the remote at the worktree head, or a reviewer's Verdict for the PR head by that dispatch. Seats without proof stay and get one digest line. `coordinator-drain.sh` also drops relayed `worker_done`. `on-worker-done.sh` writes the reviewer's owner record (`--owner`).
