guard-bash: a variable bound to a plain literal in the same command (`H=blue; ssh $H uptime`, `for h in blue green; do ssh $h uptime; done`) is now judged by its value. Only a closed grammar resolves (leading `NAME=VALUE;` lines, flat statements, at most one plain `for` loop at the end); anything else is judged as before. A loop over more than 16 plain words is denied with "split the loop". A loop over a production host is now checked as that host.
