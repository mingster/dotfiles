- Fleet watch sends are steadier and asks are easier to track (#1843). A send whose `orca` process could not start is tried once more (never after a timeout), and a pass with no sender terminal takes `--from` from the babysit or issue scan `from_terminal` in config. Each ask is logged with the UTC time it was asked, and its answer with the time it was seen and the minutes waited, in the log and in the digest. Each pass writes the open asks and the live claims (which worker is on which issue) to `elon-open-work.md` beside its state file, and `elon-startup.sh` prints it; an answered ask or an ended worker leaves the file. The elon waiter reads asks with `--full`. When `orca-reply.sh` gets answer_conflict, it fetches the ask and resends the answer in the ask's thread.
