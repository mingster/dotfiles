fleet-watch nudges a stalled worker once after 2 stall windows with no new commit, then after 3 windows stops and releases it, sets its card to archived and puts its issue back in ready-for-agent. A worker with unpushed commits or changed files is never stopped. On worker_done it moves a card the worker left behind to in-review (PR open) or completed (no PR), and sets completed on a release after merge. A failed remote transcript read is tried once more, and board-done treats archived as final.
