* fleet-watch starts one architect-pm supply worker when free seats under `work_order.target_workers` outnumber the open `ready-for-agent` issues across the watched repos. It triages `needs-triage` issues and files follow-ups from recent merged PRs and FAIL comments, each ready for an agent. At most one runs, at most once per `triage_interval_hours`, and it replaces that pass's triage worker. Each pass the digest gets `work order: N of T live, supply S` (#1689).
