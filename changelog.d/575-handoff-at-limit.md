The lead context limit hook now lets a lead at the limit hand off with `$(git rev-parse HEAD)` as the head, or after a `cd <dir> &&`, and an error in the limit can no longer skip the worker-start gate. A newline inside `$( )` is denied, a `cd` no longer ends lead detection, the handoff command in the deny text works from any folder, and `lead-handoff.sh` creates its default log folder.
