The guard hook lets a `promote <sha>` approval cover read-only inspection and Prisma client regeneration on the release host, for the hosts and app directory named in the new `promote_host_steps` key of `guard-config.json` (`app_dir`, `read_hosts`, `write_hosts`). Regeneration must follow a HEAD check or checkout of the approved sha, joined by `&&` only (`;` is refused), and a path with `..` is refused. `run-production-data-step.sh` takes only the sha and a step name. Without the key, nothing changes.
