guard-bash: one quote aware pass now reads `$'...'` (with NUL cut words), `#` comments, `$$` and the like, and subshells or brace groups after `if`, `then`, `do` or `!`, so a secret read, force push, heavy job or blocked script hidden behind them is denied. A plain mention of a blocked script (multi line quoted text, `$name`, a flat `$(...)`) is allowed, while a `$` where the program or a shell's script goes is denied. `blocked_script_reasons` in `guard-config.json` gives each blocked script its own deny sentence (#1385).
